DARTSVISION / SUPPORT

Let's get you
up to date.

A few simple steps to update the software on your DartsVision computer, refresh its AutoDarts / Winmau look, and add a way to get remote help.

Start the guide ↓

BEFORE YOU START

What you'll need

  • Your DartsVision computer turned on and connected to the internet
  • A keyboard and mouse connected to the computer
  • The computer's administrator password, if requested
  • Time to let the update finish, then restart

The images below are illustrations. Your screen may look a little different.

01

Open the terminal

On your DartsVision computer, open the application menu and look for QTerminal or Terminal. You can also try pressing Ctrl + Alt + T together.

If AutoDarts fills the screen, press Ctrl + Alt + Q to return to the desktop first.

02

Copy and run the command

Copy the entire line below. Click inside the terminal, paste it with Ctrl + Shift + V (or right-click and choose Paste), then press Enter.

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mrdavidburns/lubuntu_autodarts/main/install.sh | bash

Using another device to read this page? Open this guide on the DartsVision computer to copy the command, or carefully type the whole line.

03

Follow the prompts

If asked for a password, type the computer's administrator password and press Enter. Nothing will appear while you type—not even dots. That is normal.

If asked to choose a mode, keep your existing choice; for a dedicated DartsVision machine that opens AutoDarts automatically, choose Kiosk. Read the confirmation and follow the on-screen instructions to continue.

Keep the computer on and connected to the internet until the update finishes. If it reports an error, take a photo of the message and contact us.

04

Restart and check your board

Once the update finishes, restart the computer using its application menu. You should see the refreshed AutoDarts / Winmau branding as the computer starts and on supported desktops.

Open AutoDarts and check that you are signed in, your board connects, and your cameras and calibration work. If something doesn't look right, get in touch before playing.

HERE IF YOU NEED US

Need a hand?

The update also installs RustDesk on supported computers, with a desktop shortcut. If you need help, open RustDesk and contact us. A support person can guide you through connecting and approving a remote session. Only share access details with someone you trust.

If RustDesk isn't there, the update stops, or your board isn't working, send us a photo of any message you see.

Contact DartsVision →

RustDesk may be skipped if the computer isn't supported or its dependencies cannot be installed. View the installer instructions.
